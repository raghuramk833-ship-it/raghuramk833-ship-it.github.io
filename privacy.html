Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who uses this tool

chartforge-rclone is a private tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What it accesses

The tool uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. It can see, create, update and delete only the files it uploaded itself. It cannot see any other files in the Google Drive.

What data it handles

It uploads public cryptocurrency market data (trades, order books, prices) recorded from Binance's public data streams. It does not collect, store or process personal information about anyone.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party. Files stay in the owner's own Google Drive.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from their Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services.
